Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Area served: Kochi & nearby areas.

Important: This page is general information about how this website works. It is not legal advice. The business owner should have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before final launch.

Short version: This website itself does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. It has no accounts, no database, and no analytics. If you choose to send a booking, that message goes through your own WhatsApp, phone, or email — not through a form stored on this website.

1. What this website collects

Nothing, by itself. Browsing this website does not require you to create an account, and this website does not ask for or store your name, phone number, or address on a server.

The booking form on the home page works only in your browser. What you type stays on your device until you choose to send it. It is not submitted to, or stored by, this website, and it is never published publicly.

2. Booking through WhatsApp, phone, or email

When you tap “Send booking on WhatsApp”, your browser builds a message from what you typed and opens WhatsApp (wa.me) with that message ready for you to send. Only if you press send in WhatsApp does the business receive it. After that, your message is handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy, and by the business that receives it.

The same applies to phone calls and emails: tapping a phone number uses your phone service, and tapping the email address opens your email app. This website does not record those calls or emails.

Please do not send passwords, bank details, card numbers, or one-time codes through the booking form. The business does not need those details to confirm a booking.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels. There is no cookie consent banner because there are no cookies or tracking technologies set by this website to consent to.

If you click through to WhatsApp, your phone service, or your email provider, those services may use their own cookies or tracking under their own policies. This website does not control them.

4. Third-party services

This website does not embed Google Maps, Google Fonts, YouTube videos, social media feeds, payment providers, analytics tools, or other third-party content. It uses only system fonts and the business’s own logo, which was provided by the business for this website.

The only external services involved are the ones you choose to use:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) — only when you choose to send a booking message. WhatsApp is a trademark of its owner; linking to it does not imply endorsement by WhatsApp.
  • Telephone service — when you tap a phone number.
  • Email service — when you tap the email address.
  • Website hosting — this preview site is hosted on GitHub Pages. A hosting provider may keep standard server logs (for example, IP address and pages requested) under its own privacy policy. The final hosting provider will be confirmed before launch, and this section should be updated to name it.

5. Sharing and public exposure

This website does not sell personal information and does not publish booking requests, names, phone numbers, or messages anywhere on the site. There is no public list of customers, reviews, photos, or videos on this website. The business states it does not do video shooting or advertising of clients.

6. Security

This is a static website with no database, no user accounts, and no API keys, passwords, or secret credentials in its code. Form input is checked in your browser (for example, for a valid 10-digit Indian mobile number) and is sent only as text you choose to send via WhatsApp. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please use the contact methods you are comfortable with.

7. Your choices

You can browse without using the form at all. You can also book by calling +91 80897 95181 or +91 75919 15181 instead of using WhatsApp.

8. Changes to this policy

If how this website works changes — for example, if analytics, a contact form that stores messages, or a payment service is added later — this page must be updated before that change goes live.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or about a booking can be sent to the business at wehelp24seven@gmail.com, or by calling the numbers above. No dedicated data protection officer is named on this website, because none has been confirmed for publication.
